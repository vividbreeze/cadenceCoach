Cadence Coach

The running metronome for your step rate

Cadence Coach sets the beat for every step. Choose your target step rate in steps per minute (SPM), plan intervals with changing rates and train a higher cadence on the treadmill.

Features

  • Freely adjustable step rate (SPM)
  • Interval training with changing step rates
  • Clear audio signal for every step
  • Simple to use, even while running
  • No account, no ads, no data collection
